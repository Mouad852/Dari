# Assets

The active logo is the transparent 500×500 raster at
`apps/web/public/logo-mark.png`, used by `SiteNav.tsx`. It is the sole tracked
brand-mark asset so the product and repository do not carry competing concept
exports.

Vector source artwork, final favicon and social-image artwork, and approved
marketing photography have not been supplied. Until photography is available,
image slots use the `--sable-200` PHOTO placeholder.

The mark combines two joined rooflines. Preserve its terracotta treatment and
clear space when deriving future brand assets.
